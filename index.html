High-Throughput Spatial Simulation Engine

Memory-Bounded Environmental & GIS Migration Model

An environmental research team was running multi-year population movement simulations that crashed due to massive satellite map files and took over 4 hours per run. I redesigned how the software reads map data and evaluates locations so researchers can run accurate 48-month scenarios in under 5 minutes on standard hardware.

Impact: Cut runtime from 4+ hours to under 5 minutes, kept RAM under 500 MB on gigabyte datasets, and reduced location-lookup CPU load by 99%.
